## Findings

| Finding | Evidence | Fix | Closed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 (C8, BRD-08): the box rule "centre inside" is not asserted against "body overlaps" | No item in the test has its body inside a box and its centre outside it; the comment "the box edge cuts the chest's body but not its centre" does not match the coordinates (the chest is at 550, the box ends at 300). Mutation in `src/renderer/board/board.ts` `endBox`: `t.x >= x0 - 50 && t.x <= x1 + 50 && t.y >= y0 - 50 && t.y <= y1 + 50` (overlap for 1-cell items); rebuild -> C8 passes | add an item whose body the box covers but whose centre is outside it (for example G at (350, 150) with the box ending at 320) and assert it is not selected; also drag one box right to left | 0bba09e |
| F2 (C10, C17, BRD-10): "the others move by the same offset" is not told apart from "each snaps on its own" | Every follower is a 1-cell character on a cell centre, so snapping it gives the same place as the offset. Mutation in `board.ts` `endDrag`: each follower's move is `this.dropAt(ft.kind, ..., ft.x + rawDx, ft.y + rawDy, shift, ft.rotation)` instead of `ft.x + dx, ft.y + dy`; rebuild -> C10 passes | put an off-grid follower in the selection (an object at (333, 377) or a 2-cell character) and assert it moves by exactly the dragged item's snapped offset | 0bba09e |
| F3 (C2, BRD-02): a non-square token dragged from the campaign list snaps as if it were square | Throwaway e2e at HEAD: `tokens/alto.png` 64 x 128 with `{ tamanho: 1 }` (drawn 1 x 2 cells, `h` 200) dragged from the Tokens list to (130, 170) lands at (150, 150); BRD-01 puts the 2-cell axis on a line, (150, 200). Cause: `library.ts` calls `board.dropAt`, which uses `aspect(hash)`, and that is 1 while the board has not loaded the image yet. C2 uses only square images | take the aspect from the library entry (its image size) or load the texture before `dropAt`; add a non-square token to C2 | 0bba09e |
| F4 (C17, BRD-17): dragging a grouped item that is selected without the rest of its group is refused | Throwaway e2e at HEAD: G1+G2 grouped, G3 alone; the master's box (dragged right to left) takes G2 and G3; dragging G2 moves nothing and shows "Sem permissão", though the master may move every item. Cause: `startDrag` keeps a selection of several as it is (no group expansion), `endBox` and Shift+click add single members, and `token/moveMany` refuses a move that leaves out a member. BRD-17 says dragging an item of a group selects the whole group and moves it | expand box and Shift+click selections (or the drag's followers) to whole groups; add the case to C17 | 0bba09e |
| F5 (C18, BRD-18): a group with a member hidden from the player lets her drag her item away alone | Throwaway e2e at HEAD: Ana's Guerreira grouped with Cavalo, the master hides Cavalo; Ana drags the Guerreira from (150, 150) to (150, 350): the master sees it at (150, 350), Cavalo stays at (250, 150), no message. Cause: the player's view has no Cavalo, so the drag has no followers and sends `token/move`, and the reducer's `token/move` never checks the group (only `token/moveMany` does). Any client can also split a group with `token/move` | refuse `token/move` of an item in a group of 2+ (or route it through the moveMany group check) and have the board show "Sem permissão" for it; add the hidden-member case to C18 | 0bba09e |
